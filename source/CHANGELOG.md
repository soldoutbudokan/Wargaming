# 0.2.0 — More realistic battles

- Terrain now affects play. Rivers require crossings, ponds block movement, woodland provides cover and obscures sight, and elevation changes combat advantage.
- Added route finding around blocked terrain, shared with the drawn battlefield.
- Added fatigue, cohesion, recovery during rest, momentum in cavalry charges, and morale shocks from nearby retreats.
- Added weapon profiles for bows, slings, muskets, and cannon, with finite volleys, reloads, facing limits, and obstructed firing lanes.
- Austerlitz uses musket infantry and artillery crews; square formation is available only to musket infantry.
- Added close views of soldiers and horses, visible gun batteries, weapon effects, smoke, dust, and range indicators.
- Added a formation condition panel for morale, cohesion, fatigue, ammunition, reload progress, and terrain.
- Replay now records four times per simulated second and preserves battle effects.
- Added terrain and DOM integration tests. Browser/GPU verification remains separate.
