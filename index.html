THE FIELD GUIDE

Less to learn.
More to command.

You command the formations in teal. Make the opposing army retreat to win.

01

Select your soldiers

Click a formation on the map or its card below. Drag a box to select several. Shift-click adds to your selection.

02

Give one clear order

Click open ground to move. Click an enemy formation to attack. Use Hold to defend or Charge to close quickly.

03

Take your time

Press Space to pause and issue orders. Rest tired troops. Hills and woodland affect combat; rivers require a ford. Watch ammunition and keep firing lanes clear.

Pan by dragging with the right or middle mouse button. Touch: tap to select and command; drag empty ground to pan. Replay records your battle and lets you scrub through it.
